# Top 5 TestAS Exam Myths Students and Parents Commonly Believe

TestAS is a standardized academic aptitude test for international students seeking admission to universities in Germany or German-partner programs in Vietnam. While the test is increasingly recognized, many parents and students still encounter unverified claims, leading to misguided preparation timelines, improper study materials, or underestimating exam room time pressure.

Below are the 5 most common misconceptions along with practical insights to help students and families prepare effectively while saving time and resources.

![5 Key Facts About TestAS That Students and Parents Should Know](/images/blog/5-su-that-ve-testas.webp)

---

## 1. Using Paper TestAS practice materials for Digital TestAS because "logic is the same either way"

* **The Common Myth:** Many candidates purchase old paper-based TestAS question booklets to prepare for the computer-based (Digital) test, believing that both formats simply evaluate basic pattern logic and math.
* **The Reality:** The test interface and user experience differ fundamentally between the two formats:
  * **One-Way Navigation:** In the Digital TestAS, questions are presented one at a time. Once you select an answer and click proceed, the system locks that question permanently. You cannot return to review or change answers like on a paper test.
  * **No Physical Scratch Paper:** Computer test centers do not provide scratch paper. Candidates must adapt to mental math or navigate the on-screen digital scratchpad using a mouse.
* **Practical Advice:** If you are registered for Digital TestAS, you must practice directly on computer software to build mouse dexterity, get used to question locking, and manage on-screen time constraints.

## 2. You must finish 12th grade before taking TestAS

* **The Common Myth:** Most families assume candidates must complete high school, obtain a diploma, or wait until the second semester of 12th grade before registering for TestAS.
* **The Reality:** The TestDaF Institute enforces no age minimum. Students in **11th grade** or early 12th grade are fully eligible to take the test.
* **Why 11th Grade is the Ideal Time:**
  * **Permanent Validity:** TestAS certificates never expire. Scores obtained during 11th grade remain completely valid for university applications years later.
  * **Ample Retake Window:** If your initial score falls short of competitive scholarship cutoffs, you still have plenty of time to review and retake the test in 12th grade without jeopardizing APS or visa deadlines.
  * **Reduced Senior Year Stress:** In 12th grade, students must balance national high school exams and language certificates (IELTS or TestDaF). Having TestAS secured early removes a huge burden.

## 3. Taking TestAS at Vietnamese-German University (VGU) only counts internally for VGU

* **The Common Myth:** Because VGU hosts its own admissions test sessions on schedules differing from global testas.de test dates, some assume the resulting certificate is only recognized within VGU.
* **The Reality:** TestAS certificates issued through VGU tests carry identical legal standing and full recognition throughout Germany.
  * VGU is an intergovernmental public university partnership between Vietnam and the state of Hesse, Germany. It is officially authorized by German testing authorities (ITB Consulting and g@me e.V.) to administer TestAS sessions.
  * Test contents, administration standards, and scoring certificates are managed directly by the TestAS Institute in Germany.
  * You can submit your VGU-administered TestAS certificate for APS certificate screening, student visa applications, and direct university applications across Germany.

*Check admission procedures:* [How to Apply to Vietnamese-German University (VGU) with TestAS](/en/blog/xet-tuyen-vgu-bang-testas).

## 4. TestAS preparation requires 6 to 12 months just like IELTS

* **The Common Myth:** Seeing multiple exam sections conducted entirely in a foreign language, many families assume a full year of intensive tutoring is required.
* **The Reality:** TestAS does not measure long-term vocabulary acquisition or academic memorization like language proficiency exams. It evaluates existing logical thinking and data processing speeds.
  * For candidates with solid quantitative foundations and functional English skills (equivalent to IELTS 5.5–6.0 for reading comprehension), the optimal study window is **6 to 8 weeks (around 2 months)**.
  * This window is sufficient to master question formats, dissect reasoning patterns, and practice timed simulations.
  * Stretching preparation over an entire year is unnecessary and often leads to candidate burnout.

*Check the standard preparation strategy:* [TestAS Preparation Guide from A–Z: Study Roadmap and Time Allocation](/en/blog/kinh-nghiem-thi-testas).

## 5. "Strong math students with high IQ do not need preparation"

* **The Common Myth:** Students excelling in high school math or science tracks often assume the test features simple arithmetic and visual logic that require no advance practice.
* **The Reality:** The decisive challenge in TestAS is not conceptual difficulty, but **EXTREME TIME PRESSURE**.
  * The test presents a high volume of questions with an average of only **70 to 90 seconds per question**. Within this narrow window, you must read academic English prompts, filter distractor data, detect logic rules, and choose the correct answer.
  * Without prior conditioning, even skilled math students easily get trapped attempting complex problems, wasting 4–5 minutes while leaving subsequent easy questions unanswered.
  * Top percentile TestAS scores stem from disciplined time allocation tactics and rapid pattern recognition, not just raw mathematical aptitude.
