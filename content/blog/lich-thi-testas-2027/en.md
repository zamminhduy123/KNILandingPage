# Official TestAS Exam Dates 2027: Fees, Exam Structure, and Registration Guide

Scores hold lifetime validity, serving as an indispensable requirement for APS verification for studying in Germany and enabling direct admission and talent scholarships at [Vietnamese-German University (VGU)](/en/blog/xet-tuyen-vgu-bang-testas).

## 2. What is TestAS and Why is it Essential?

TestAS is a standardized cognitive aptitude examination designed for international students seeking higher education in Germany. The exam does not evaluate memorized factual knowledge or historical dates. Instead, it measures logical deduction, numerical analysis, chart interpretation, and spatial reasoning in an academic language (English or German).

### Core Functions Across Two Pathways:

1. **German Study Abroad Route:** The Academic Evaluation Centre (APS) at the German Embassy in Hanoi mandates TestAS scores for high school graduates before issuing certificates for Studienkolleg preparatory courses or direct undergraduate admission.
2. **Vietnamese-German University (VGU) Route:** VGU relies on TestAS as an independent entrance exam. High scores secure direct admission and merit scholarships ranging from **25%, 50%, to 100% of tuition**.

## 3. Comparing Paper-based and Digital TestAS

Examinees may choose between paper and digital formats. Key differences include:

| Criteria | Paper-based TestAS | Digital TestAS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Test Duration** | Approx. 4.5 hours (including breaks). | Approx. 3.5 hours (including breaks). |
| **Pacing Architecture** | Section-level timing; candidates distribute time freely across items. | **Per-item countdown clock (70–90 seconds/question). Automatic lock on timeout.** |
| **Available Modules** | 4 traditional modules: Economics, Engineering, Math/CS, Humanities. | 6 modules: adding dedicated tracks for **Medicine** and **Life Sciences**. |
| **Score Metric** | Standard Score from **70 to 130** (Benchmark mean: 100). | Numerical TestAS Score from **0 to 200**. |
| **Navigation Flexibility** | Able to revisit and modify earlier answers within the same section. | **No backtracking; locked questions cannot be revisited.** |

## 4. Comprehensive Breakdown of TestAS Exam Sections

The examination comprises two compulsory components:

![Detailed TestAS Exam Structure for Core Test and Subject Modules](/images/blog/cau-truc-de-thi-testas-chi-tiet.webp)

### Part 1: Core Test (Kerntest)

Assesses general cognitive reasoning over **110 minutes** across 4 subtests:
1. **Solving Quantitative Problems (22 questions / 45 minutes):** Arithmetic reasoning, percentage proportions, practical mathematical modeling.
2. **Inferring Relations (22 questions / 10 minutes):** Determining verbal analogies between academic concept pairs.
3. **Completing Number Series (22 questions / 25 minutes):** Identifying computational rules governing sequential series.
4. **Continuing Numerical Patterns (22 questions / 30 minutes):** Recognizing algorithmic relationships within matrix schemas.

### Part 2: Subject-Specific Module

Candidates select 1 specialized module matching their prospective degree (145 – 150 minutes):
* **Economics Module:** Analyzing Economic Interrelations and Analyzing Processes.
* **Engineering Module:** Formalizing Technical Interrelations, Visualizing Solids, and Analyzing Technical Interrelations.
* **Mathematics, CS and Natural Sciences Module:** Analyzing Scientific Interrelations and Understanding Formal Depictions.
* **Humanities and Social Sciences Module:** Understanding and Interpreting Texts and Flexible Use of Language.

## 5. Step-by-Step Guide to Registering for TestAS 2027

Follow these five steps on the international examination portal to secure your seat:

![Step-by-step Registration Guide for TestAS 2027 on Official Portal](/images/blog/huong-dan-cach-dang-ky-thi-testas-2027.webp)

* **Step 1: Create an Account:** Visit [www.testas.de](https://www.testas.de), select *Participants*, and input identity details matching your passport.
* **Step 2: Select Exam Parameters:** Choose your 2027 test date, delivery format (Paper or Digital), medium (English or German), and subject module.
* **Step 3: Select Test Centre:**
  * *Northern Vietnam:* Vietnamese-German Centre (VDZ) at HUST Hanoi.
  * *Southern Vietnam:* Goethe-Institut Ho Chi Minh City or Vietnamese-German University (VGU).
* **Step 4: Finalize Payment:** Pay the 150 Euro international fee via Visa or Mastercard enabled for online transactions.
* **Step 5: Print Admission Ticket:** 1–2 weeks before test day, log into your profile to print your official *Admission Ticket*, which must be presented alongside your original passport.

## 7. How to Interpret Your Standardized Score Report

TestAS does not issue pass or fail verdicts. Instead, performance is scored through normalized psychometrics:

1. **Standard Score (Standardwert):** Ranges from **70 to 130**, with **100** anchored as the international benchmark mean. Scores between 90 and 110 reflect average performance; marks above 110 denote superior cognitive ability.
2. **Percentile Rank (Prozentrang):** Scales from **1 to 100**, indicating the proportion of global examinees who scored equal to or below your mark.
3. **Language Competency Evaluation:** Reflects your operational language command according to the CEFR framework (typically B1, B2, or C1).

1. **Practice with Official g.a.s.t. Samples:** Download authentic test sets from [testas.de](https://www.testas.de) to understand item formats.
2. **Master Strict Time Allocation:** The primary obstacle for candidates is pacing. In *Inferring Relations* (22 items in 10 minutes), you have under 28 seconds per question. Time all practice sessions rigorously.
3. **Internalize Academic Vocabulary:** Master math and science terminology in English or German (*prime numbers*, *matrices*, *inversely proportional*, *friction*, *derivatives*). Rapid comprehension saves precious seconds.
4. **Use Elimination Strategies:** In complex 3D solid visualization items, disproving three incorrect options is often vastly quicker than constructing the exact solution mentally.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How many times can I retake TestAS?
You can retake TestAS without any limit. Each sitting requires the standard 150 Euro registration fee. When applying to universities, you may submit your highest score certificate.

### 2. How long are TestAS scores valid?
TestAS certificates never expire. Scores are valid indefinitely. You can sit the test in Grade 11 and retain your score permanently for university applications.

### 3. Can Grade 11 or Grade 12 students take TestAS?
Yes. There are no age restrictions. Taking TestAS in Grade 11 alleviates senior-year stress. Grade 12 students should sit the February or March 2027 sessions to meet German Winter Semester deadlines.

### 4. Is the English version valued less than the German version?
No. Both languages hold equal merit in evaluations conducted by the APS, German universities, and VGU. Always select the language in which you perform best.
