## 2. The Pitfalls of Free Sample Materials

Many candidates enter the testing room shocked to find actual questions far more demanding than free online samples. This discrepancy stems from two main causes:

* **The Illusion of Illustrative Samples:** Free online resources primarily introduce question types. Reasoning depth and distractor complexity are intentionally diluted for accessibility.
* **The Reality of Time Pressure:** The Core Test demands solving items with an average allowance of **~70 seconds per question**.
* Without rapid pattern-recognition reflexes, complex problems burn 2 to 3 minutes each, cascading into missed answers across subsequent sections.

At KNI, our students train against real exam pacing and distractor complexity from their very first session.
