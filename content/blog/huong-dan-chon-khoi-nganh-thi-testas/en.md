# How to Choose Your TestAS Subject Module: Detailed Guide for VGU and German Universities

Selecting the wrong Subject-Specific Module in the TestAS examination is a critical error that can invalidate your university application at the preliminary screening stage. TestAS is not a one-size-fits-all test: in addition to the compulsory Core Test, you must register for the precise academic field corresponding to your prospective degree program in Germany or at [Vietnamese-German University (VGU)](/en/blog/xet-tuyen-vgu-bang-testas).

This guide delivers an in-depth breakdown of each subject module, provides a comprehensive cognitive matrix, and features an exact major lookup table so you can choose correctly from day one.

![Comprehensive Guide to Choosing Your TestAS Subject Module for VGU and German Universities](/images/blog/huong-dan-chon-khoi-nganh-testas.webp)

## 1. Overview: What Subject Modules Does TestAS Offer?

Every TestAS sitting contains two distinct parts: the **Core Test** measuring general analytical reasoning, and the **Subject-Specific Module** evaluating cognitive aptitude in your chosen academic discipline.

The number of available subject modules depends on the testing format you select:

![Subject Module Differences Between Paper TestAS and Digital TestAS](/images/blog/su-khac-biet-giua-digital-va-paper-testas.webp)

* **Paper-based TestAS (Written):** Offers **4 traditional modules**: Economics; Engineering; Mathematics, Computer Science & Natural Sciences; and Humanities & Social Sciences.
* **Digital TestAS (Computer-based):** Features **6 specialized modules**, introducing dedicated tracks for **Medicine** and **Life Sciences**.

## 3. In-Depth Breakdown of Each Subject Module

### 3.1. Economics Module

This module measures quantitative reasoning, supply-and-demand modeling, and optimal decision-making under constrained business scenarios.

* **Paper Format Structure (2 subtests – 44 questions / 145 minutes):**
  * *Analyzing Economic Interrelations (22 questions / 65 minutes):* Interpret multi-tiered tables, economic charts, and identify correlation trends among macroeconomic variables.
  * *Analyzing Processes (22 questions / 80 minutes):* Formulate flowcharts and solve optimization problems for production pipelines, supply chains, or employee scheduling.
* **Applicable Degree Programs:**
  * **At VGU:** Business Administration (BBA), Finance and Accounting (BFA), Economics (BSE), Digital Business and International Management (BDE).
  * **In Germany:** Business Administration (*BWL*), Economics (*VWL*), International Management, Auditing, Supply Chain Logistics.

---

### 3.2. Engineering Module

This module assesses spatial imagination and intuitive physical mechanics—essential competencies for prospective engineers.

* **Paper Format Structure (3 subtests – 66 questions / 150 minutes):**
  * *Formalizing Technical Interrelations (22 questions / 60 minutes):* Translate physical laws (levers, fluid pressures, electrical circuits) into logical algebraic formulations.
  * *Visualizing Solids (22 questions / 30 minutes):* Mentally manipulate 3D geometric solids across multi-angle orthographic projections or predict cross-section slices.
  * *Analyzing Technical Interrelations (22 questions / 60 minutes):* Evaluate kinematics and mechanical systems (gear trains, pulley blocks, hydraulic pistons) to deduce force directions and velocities.
* **Applicable Degree Programs:**
  * **At VGU:** Mechanical Engineering (MEN), Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), Civil Engineering (BCE), Architecture (ARC), Mechatronics (MEC), Biomedical Engineering (BME).
  * **In Germany:** Engineering faculties (*Ingenieurwissenschaften*): Mechanical (*Maschinenbau*), Electrical (*Elektrotechnik*), Automotive, Aerospace, Civil, Architecture.

---

### 3.3. Mathematics, Computer Science and Natural Sciences Module

Evaluates pure algorithmic thinking and mathematical deduction, independent of prior programming experience.

* **Paper Format Structure (2 subtests – 44 questions / 145 minutes):**
  * *Analyzing Scientific Interrelations (22 questions / 60 minutes):* Comprehend empirical scientific texts and experimental graphs across physics, chemistry, and biology to deduce valid conclusions.
  * *Understanding Formal Depictions (22 questions / 85 minutes):* Decipher complex algorithmic flowcharts represented in structured pseudocode. You route multi-variable data inputs through conditional decision gates to ascertain correct terminal outputs.
* **Applicable Degree Programs:**
  * **At VGU:** Compulsory for Computer Science (CSE).
  * **In Germany:** MINT disciplines (*Mathematik, Informatik, Naturwissenschaften*): Computer Science (*Informatik*), Data Science, Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, Pure Chemistry.

---

### 3.4. Humanities, Cultural Studies and Social Sciences Module

Measures verbal critical reasoning and the ability to dissect arguments within high-density academic texts.

* **Paper Format Structure (2 subtests – 44 questions / 90 minutes):**
  * *Understanding and Interpreting Texts (22 questions / 45 minutes):* Read short philosophical, historical, or cultural essays to synthesize core theses or detect logical fallacies.
  * *Flexible Use of Language (22 questions / 45 minutes):* Complete fill-in-the-blank contextual items, rephrase syntactic structures, and choose precise contextual academic synonyms.
* **Applicable Degree Programs:**
  * **At VGU:** Currently VGU does not offer standalone humanities degrees.
  * **In Germany:** Linguistics, Law (*Jura*), Psychology (*Psychologie*), Journalism, Philosophy, International Relations, Sociology.

---

### 3.5 & 3.6. Medicine & Life Sciences Modules

These two tracks are administered exclusively within **Digital TestAS**, serving competitive admissions criteria at German medical and healthcare faculties:

* **Distinctive Exam Features:**
  * *Clinical Reasoning:* Analyze medical records, diagnostic indicators, and therapeutic guidelines to determine logical clinical hypotheses.
  * *Memory and Retention:* The software displays cell anatomy diagrams and molecular structures for brief memorization intervals. Data is then hidden, and you answer detailed queries regarding the displayed material.
* **Applicable Degree Programs in Germany:**
  * **Medicine Track:** Compulsory for Human Medicine (*Humanmedizin*), Dentistry (*Zahnmedizin*), Pharmacy (*Pharmazie*), Veterinary Medicine (*Tiermedizin*).
  * **Life Sciences Track:** Biotechnology (*Biotechnologie*), Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, Environmental Agriscience.

## 5. Three High-Scoring Preparation Tactics for Subject Modules

1. **Prioritize methodology over memorization:** The Engineering exam never requires calculus derivations; the Economics test never quizzes arcane financial formulas. The test measures how you interpret analytical models. Practice with official sample tests from g.a.s.t. to acclimatize to item archetypes.
2. **Master academic field vocabulary early:** Whether testing in English or German, unfamiliarity with basic mathematical and engineering syntax (*proportional*, *inversely proportional*, *clockwise*, *flowchart*, *cross-section*) squanders precious reading minutes. Compile a specialized glossary during your first study week.
3. **Simulate continuous testing under strict time limits:** Subject modules span 90 minutes (digital) to 150 minutes (paper). Train with strict timers at home to build cognitive endurance and sustain peak concentration through the final question.
