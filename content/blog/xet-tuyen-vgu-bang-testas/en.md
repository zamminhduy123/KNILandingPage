# Applying to VGU with TestAS: Complete Guide to Criteria, Process, and Scholarships 2026

Vietnamese-German University (VGU) admits students via TestAS through its dedicated entrance examination (Mode 1) or by submitting existing international TestAS certificates (Mode 4). Composite admission scores are calculated using a weighted formula: 40% Core Test plus 60% Subject-Specific Module. To qualify for admission, candidates must achieve a minimum of 50 points on each Digital TestAS component, satisfy English language prerequisites, and earn at least 15.00/30.00 across 3 national high school graduation exam subjects (including Mathematics and Literature).

This pathway enables 12th-grade students to secure early admission offers by May or June, while opening opportunities for merit scholarships covering up to 100% of first-year tuition.

---

## 1. What is VGU and Why Does It Use TestAS for Admissions?

Vietnamese-German University (VGU) is an intergovernmental public university founded upon a bilateral agreement between Vietnam and the Federal Republic of Germany. Academic curricula adhere to rigorous German standards through partnering German universities. Instruction is conducted entirely in English.

Rather than relying solely on high school grade transcripts or graduation exam scores, VGU employs TestAS (Test for Academic Studies) as its cornerstone evaluation metric for three reasons:

1. **Assessing Independent Analytical Aptitude:** TestAS evaluates deductive reasoning, scientific problem-solving, and quantitative analysis in English rather than memorized theory.
2. **Early, Independent Admission:** Testing takes place in May and June 2026. Admission results are independent of national high school exam schedules, offering early peace of mind well before July.
3. **Transparent Merit Scholarships:** TestAS scores directly determine 25%, 50%, and 100% first-year tuition waivers for incoming freshmen.

To understand basic test structure, fees, and scoring rubrics, review our foundational guide: [What is TestAS? Exam Structure, Fees, and Registration Guide 2026](/en/blog/testas-la-gi).

## 2. Five Admission Modes at VGU for 2026

For 2026, VGU offers 1,195 enrollment quotas across 13 undergraduate degree programs through five independent pathways:

* **Mode 1 - Dedicated Digital TestAS Examination:** Candidates sit computer-based exams administered on VGU campuses in May or June 2026.
* **Mode 2 - High School Transcript Evaluation (GPA):** Open to 2026 high school graduates with grade 10, 11, and 12 Math GPAs $\ge$ 7.00 and overall GPAs $\ge$ 7.00. Applications open April 1 to July 14, 2026.
* **Mode 3 - Direct Admission:** For winners of national/international academic competitions and ISEF science fairs. Application deadline: July 14, 2026.
* **Mode 4 - International Diplomas and Certificates:** Review of SAT, ACT, IB, A-Levels, WACE, or pre-existing global TestAS certificates.
* **Mode 5 - National High School Graduation Exam 2026:** Based on 3-subject exam combinations. Applications accepted June 1 to July 14, 2026.

Mode 1 offers the greatest personal control and early confirmation.

## 3. Step-by-Step VGU Application Process via TestAS

To register successfully, complete steps across both VGU and official TestAS platforms:

![VGU TestAS Application and Registration Procedure](/images/blog/quy-trinh-dang-ky-xet-tuyen-vgu.webp)

### Step 1: Prepare Digital Documentation
Prepare clear scanned copies of the following documents:
* Valid National ID card or Passport (must present original on test day).
* High school diploma or provisional graduation certificate (for graduated candidates).
* Valid international English certificate (IELTS Academic $\ge$ 5.0 or equivalent, if available).

### Step 2: Register on VGU's Online Portal
Visit [apply.vgu.edu.vn](https://apply.vgu.edu.vn), create an account, fill in degree program preferences, and upload your digital documents.

### Step 3: Register on the Global TestAS Portal
Visit [testas.de](https://www.testas.de), register a candidate profile, and book your examination session.

*Note for May 16, 2026 session:* You only need to register on [apply.vgu.edu.vn](https://apply.vgu.edu.vn). VGU will send detailed payment instructions via email.

### Step 4: Verify English Proficiency
Candidates without IELTS 5.0+ are scheduled for the 4-skill VGU ETEST English examination organized during the same test cycle.

## 5. Scoring Formula and Admission Thresholds

### 1. Composite Score Calculation

$$\text{Composite Score} = (\text{Core Test Score} \times 0.4) + (\text{Subject Module Score} \times 0.6)$$

### 2. Four Mandatory Admission Conditions

![4 Admission Criteria for VGU Entry via TestAS 2026](/images/blog/xet-tuyen-vgu-bang-testas-2026.webp)

Candidates must simultaneously satisfy 4 criteria:

1. **Component Floor Scores:** At least **50 points** on each Digital TestAS section (or $\ge$ **90 points** per section for paper-based submissions under Mode 4).
2. **Graduation Exam Minimum:** Total of at least **15.00/30.00** across 3 high school graduation subjects (Math and Literature required) per Ministry of Education guidelines.
3. **English Proficiency:** IELTS Academic $\ge$ 5.0, VGU ETEST $\ge$ 75/100, or graduation from an English-medium international high school.
4. **Program Cutoff Score:** Equal or exceed program-specific quota cutoff thresholds announced by the Admissions Council.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Can current 12th graders without high school diplomas register for VGU TestAS?
Yes. Current 12th graders are encouraged to sit May or June sessions. You submit national ID and current transcripts, later supplementing provisional graduation certificates after national exams.

### 2. If my TestAS score is unsatisfactory, can I apply via another mode?
Yes. VGU operates five independent modes. Unsatisfactory TestAS scores do not penalize your eligibility for GPA transcript admission (Mode 2) or graduation exam admission (Mode 5).

### 3. How long are TestAS certificates valid, and can they be used in Germany?
TestAS certificates never expire and hold full international recognition. Scores are fully valid for APS certificates, German student visas, and direct university applications across Germany. See our [Comprehensive TestAS Guide](/en/blog/cam-nang-testas-toan-dien) for details.

### 4. What language is the Digital TestAS at VGU conducted in?
All exams administered at VGU are conducted entirely in English. The exam evaluates academic aptitude, logic, and data analysis rather than English grammar.

### 5. Can I register if I do not yet have an IELTS score?
Yes. Simply indicate this during registration. VGU will schedule the 4-skill VGU ETEST exam (fee: 1,000,000 VND). Scoring $\ge$ 75/100 fulfills the language prerequisite.
